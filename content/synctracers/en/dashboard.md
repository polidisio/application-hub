## Dashboard

On iOS you can **pull down** to sync. The cards, from top to bottom:

### Apple Health status

The connection status, the total number of stored workouts, a *Syncing…* indicator while it works and the time of the last copy to the Vault (with a red notice if the copy to iCloud Drive failed).

### Summary

| Card | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Workouts that started today. |
| **This Week** | Workouts since the start of the week (following your region's first weekday). |
| **Calories** | Sum of kcal for this week's workouts. A workout with no calories counts as 0. |
| **Duration** | Total time this week, as "Xh Ym". |

### Streak

- **Current:** consecutive days with at least one workout, of any type, counting back from today. If you haven't worked out yet today, it counts from yesterday: the streak only breaks after a full day without a workout.
- **Longest:** the longest streak in your whole history, with no cap.

If you've worked out today you'll see "You worked out today! 🔥"; otherwise, the date of your last workout.

### Recent Workouts

The 5 most recent: type, date and time, duration, kcal if available and distance if above 0 (in km or metres).

### Empty states

- **No Workouts:** Apple Health connected but no data. Includes the **Sync Now** button.
- **HealthKit not connected:** go to Settings to connect it.

## Workouts

The tab shows **all** stored workouts, newest first. Each row has an icon, type, date and time, duration, kcal and distance.

- **Search** (iOS only): by type name, in the current language (*Search by type*).
- **Refresh button** in the bar: runs a sync.
- Rows are read-only: they can't be opened, edited or deleted.

## Workout types

The app reads all Apple Health workouts. 18 types have their own name and the rest are stored as **Other**:

Running, Cycling, Swimming, Walking, Hiking, Yoga, Strength Training, Functional Training, Dancing (social dance only), Elliptical, Rowing, Stair Climbing, Pilates, Kickboxing, Surfing, Tennis, Soccer and Basketball.

Cross training, HIIT, mixed cardio, cardio dance and barre, among others, appear as **Other**.
