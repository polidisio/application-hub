TrackHobbies is your personal leisure journal: log the **books** you read, the **series** you watch and the **games** you play, and track your progress in each one. There are no accounts or sign-up: open the app and start.

## Requirements

- An iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later.
- The app is available in English and Spanish and follows your device language.
- To sync between devices you need to be signed in to iCloud. Without iCloud the app works the same, but your data stays on that device.
- Searching for titles needs a connection. Adding by hand always works, even offline.

## First launch

The first time you open the app you'll see a sheet titled **Help improve TrackHobbies**. It asks whether to share anonymous usage data (which features you use). Your titles, notes, reviews and ratings are never sent.

- **Share anonymous usage** turns analytics on.
- **Not now** leaves it off.

You can't dismiss the sheet without choosing. You can change your mind at any time in the **Stats** tab (the *Share anonymous usage* switch).

## A tour of the app

The bottom bar has four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Books** | Your book library. The app opens on this tab. |
| **Series** | The series you watch. |
| **Games** | The video games you play. |
| **Stats** | Statistics, iCloud status, backup and credits. |

Books, series and games work the same way: a list grouped by status, a **+** button to add, and search and filters at the top. There is no settings screen: everything else lives in **Stats**.

## Statuses

Every title has a status that says where it stands:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Wishlist** | You want to get to it someday. |
| **Not started** | You have it, but haven't begun. |
| **In progress** | You're reading, watching or playing it. |
| **Completed** | Finished. This is where you can rate and review. |
| **Archived** | Finished and set aside from the main view. Counts as completed in the stats. |

> If the app can't open your database, you'll see **Couldn't load your data**. The app runs in temporary mode: anything you add won't be saved. Your previous data is still on the device; close and reopen the app, and update it if the problem continues.
