title: Tracking progress
order: 3
---

Tap a title in the list to open its detail screen. From top to bottom you'll see the cover, title and author, the **Progress** card (with the status menu), tracking (only while *In progress*), **Dates**, **Rating**, **Summary** and **Time spent**.

## Changing the status

In the **Progress** card open the status menu and pick one of the five. The app adjusts the dates for you:

| Change | Effect on dates |
|---|---|
| To **In progress** | Sets the start date to today, if it had none. |
| To **Completed** | Sets the end date to today. |
| **Completed** to **In progress** | Clears the end date. |
| To **Wishlist** or **Not started** | Clears **both** dates. |

If you move a title out of *Completed* to anything other than *Archived* and it has a rating, the app warns you: **Remove from “Completed”?** Your rating will be deleted. Going from *Completed* to *Archived* keeps the rating.

## Books

The **Reading progress** section has a **Mode** selector:

- **By pages**: type the **Current page**. Tap **Total pages** to change the total (only if your edition has a different count). The total must be between 1 and 10,000 and can't be lower than the current page. If you type a page above the total, it's capped at the total. A bar shows *Page x / y (z%)*.
- **By percentage**: a 0–100 slider.

**Switching mode** asks for confirmation (**Change tracking mode?**) because it deletes the other mode's data: going to pages deletes the percentage; going to percentage deletes the current page and total pages.

**Auto-complete:** reaching the last page (or 100% in percentage mode) moves the book to *Completed* and sets the end date to today. A **Mark as completed** button also appears. It only triggers when you change the page or the slider, not when you edit the total.

## Series

The **Tracking** section has two steppers: **Season** (S2 of 5) and **Episode** (E3 of 20). The minimum is 1; the maximum is the stored total or, if there is none, 99 seasons and 999 episodes. In the list, a series in progress shows its S/E progress.

Series **don't complete on their own**: change the status by hand.

## Games

The **Tracking** section has the **Hours played** field (decimals accepted). Games don't auto-complete either. When the game isn't *In progress*, hours also show in **Time spent**, as a whole number.

## Dates

The **Dates** card shows **Start** and **End** and, below, the days between them (*N days*). A date only appears if it already exists: there's no way to add a date by hand to a title that has none. Books imported from Goodreads only carry an end date, so they show no duration.

## Archiving and deleting

- **Archive:** in the *Completed* section, swipe a row **right** and tap **Archive**. It's only available for completed items. To un-archive, change the status in the detail screen. The *Archived* section is collapsed by default.
- **Delete:** swipe a row **left** and tap **Delete**. You'll see **Delete “title”?**: progress, rating and comment are lost and it can't be undone. Deletion also syncs to your other devices. There is no delete button inside the detail screen.
