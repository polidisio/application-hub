## The list

Each tab groups titles into collapsible sections by status, in this order: **Wishlist**, **Not started**, **In progress**, **Completed** and **Archived**. Only sections that have items are shown, and *Archived* starts collapsed. Inside each section the order is fixed: most recently updated first. There are no sorting options.

Each row summarises progress: a book in progress shows *Page a/b* with a bar (or its percentage), a series shows a snippet of its summary and a game shows *Nh played*. The date appears as *Since X* or *X - Y*.

## Search

The search bar sits in the navigation bar (pull the list down if you don't see it). It searches by **title** and also by **author**, in all three tabs.

## Filters

The **Filters** button (lines-in-a-circle icon) opens an options panel. The icon fills in when a filter is active. Filters combine (all of them must match):

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Multi-select: **All** and the five statuses. |
| **Rating** | **All**, 2+, 3+, 4+ or 4.5+. It's a minimum; unrated titles count as 0 and are excluded. |
| **Date** | **All**, **This week**, **This month**, **This year** or **1+ year ago**. It compares the start date or, if there is none, the last-updated date. |

While a search or filter is active, the list becomes flat (no sections), shows *N results* and a **Clear filters** button. If nothing matches you'll see *No results*.

## Toolbar

- **Books:** import CSV (from Goodreads or TrackHobbies), **Filters**, export CSV and **+**. At the top left, a counter with your total books.
- **Series and Games:** import CSV, export CSV, **Filters** and **+**.

An empty tab shows *No books / series / games* and the hint *Tap + to add your first book*.
