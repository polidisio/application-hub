The **Rating** card appears in the detail screen when the title is **Completed** or **Archived**.

## Setting a rating

Tap the stars. There are 5, and the rating snaps to the nearest **quarter star** based on where you tap within the star (for example 4.25 or 4.75). The minimum is 0.25 and the maximum is 5. There's no slider or stepper: it's a direct tap on the stars. With VoiceOver you can adjust it in 0.25 steps.

> **Important:** a rating can't be cleared with a tap. The only way to lose it is to move the title out of *Completed* to a status other than *Archived*; the app asks for confirmation first (**Change and delete rating**).

## Writing a review

Below the stars is the **Comment** field (*Write a comment…*), 3 to 6 lines. List rows that have a review show an icon so you can tell which ones do.

## Where the rating shows up

- In the list, each rated row shows a star and the number with one decimal.
- In **Stats** it feeds the average rating, the top rated and the ratings breakdown. See [Statistics](/apps/trackhobbies/docs/stats).
- You can filter by minimum rating. See [Search and filters](/apps/trackhobbies/docs/search-filters).
