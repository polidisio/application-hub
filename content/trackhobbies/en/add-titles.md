There are two ways to add a title: search for it (the app fills in the cover and details) or type it in by hand.

## Search and add

1. In the Books, Series or Games tab, tap **+** (top right). The **Add Book / Series / Game** sheet opens.
2. Type in **Search by title…**. The search runs on its own a moment after you stop typing. While it works you'll see *Searching…*.
3. Each result has two buttons:
   - **Orange bookmark**: adds it as **Wishlist**.
   - **Green plus**: adds it as **Not started**.
4. The sheet closes once you add.

If nothing matches you'll see *No results found*. You need to type at least 2 characters.

### What gets saved

| Type | Data from the search | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Books | Title, author, cover, summary and total pages | Google Books (up to 20 results) |
| Series | Title, cover and summary. Total seasons and episodes arrive a moment later, if the service responds | TVmaze |
| Games | Title and cover | IGDB (up to 20 results) |

## Choosing another edition of a book

If a book was added without a page count, its detail screen shows a **Find pages** button.

1. Tap **Find pages**. **Choose edition** opens.
2. Each row shows the title and "Author · N pages". Only editions with a page count are listed.
3. When you pick one, the app fills in **only what's empty**: total pages, cover, summary and identifier. It never overwrites what you already had. The page total is rejected if it's lower than your current page.

If there are no editions with pages you'll see *No editions with page counts found.*

## Adding by hand

In the add sheet tap **Manual** (top right); the button becomes **Add**.

- **Information**: **Title** (required — *Add* stays disabled while it's empty) and **Author (optional)**, books only.
- **More details (optional)**, a collapsed group:
  - **Status**: Not started, Wishlist, In progress or Completed.
  - Books: **Total pages**. Series: **Seasons** and **Episodes**. Games: **Hours played** (decimals and comma accepted).
  - **Upload cover** (from your photo library), **Change cover**, or the trash icon to remove it.

Adding by hand with the *In progress* status sets the start date to today; with *Completed*, the end date to today. Cover photos are shrunk to 480 px and saved as JPEG.

> **Heads up:** you can't edit an item's title or author after adding it. If you get it wrong, delete it and create it again.
