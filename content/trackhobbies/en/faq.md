## I can't search for titles

Search needs a connection. If it fails you'll see a screen with a **Retry** button and one of these messages:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| No connection, or a very slow one | Check your network and try again. |
| Too many searches in a row | Wait a moment and retry. |
| The service isn't responding right now | Try again later. |
| Unexpected response from the service | Retry; if it persists, get in touch by email. |
| The service rejected the request / Search isn't configured in this build | Update the app. |

Meanwhile you can add by hand: it works offline.

## I got the title or author wrong

They can't be edited afterwards. Delete the item (swipe left → **Delete**) and create it again.

## I can't remove a rating

The rating is deleted when you move the title out of *Completed* to a status other than *Archived*. The app asks for confirmation.

## I don't see the rating or the stars

The **Rating** card only appears with the **Completed** or **Archived** status.

## A book has no page count

Open its detail screen and tap **Find pages** to pick an edition, or tap **Total pages** if it's already in progress and you want to type it in.

## It doesn't sync with my other device

Check the status in **Stats**. If it says *No iCloud account*, sign in to iCloud in the device settings. Both devices must use the same iCloud account. Offline, changes wait and sync when the network returns.

## Series don't show total seasons or episodes

Those totals arrive after you add the series and don't always exist, especially for ongoing series. You can keep using the season and episode counters without a total.

## Can I move my data to another iPhone?

Yes: with iCloud it syncs on its own. If you'd rather do it by hand, use **Export backup (JSON)** in **Stats** and **Restore backup** on the other device.

## What data is sent?

Nothing leaves your device except searches (the text goes to TVmaze, Google Books and IGDB) and, **only if you accept**, anonymous usage counters. Details are in the [privacy policy](/privacy/trackhobbies).
