The **Stats** tab (screen title **Statistics**) summarises your collection. The cards appear in this order:

## Overview

The total number of items in your collection and a card each for books, series and games.

## By status

How many titles are in each of the five statuses, and the percentage of **Completed** out of the total (it adds *Completed* and *Archived*).

## Books

- **Read**: completed plus archived.
- **Pages read**: total pages of completed or archived books (or the current page if they have no total), plus the current page of books in progress.
- **Average rating** (only if any rating exists) and **Top rated**.

## Series

**Completed**, **Seasons watched** and **Episodes watched** (totals of completed series), average rating and top rated.

## Games

**Completed**, **Hours played** (the sum over **all** games, whatever their status, in whole hours), average rating and top rated.

## Activity

- **Completed this month** and **Completed this year**.
- **Average time**: days from start to end, where both dates exist.
- **Last 30 days**: how many you've completed.

These figures count by **end date**, across all three types.

## Ratings

The **overall average** and a breakdown in four buckets: 1-2, 2-3, 3-4 and 4-5. Ratings below 1 (0.25 to 0.75) don't fall into any bucket.

## More in this tab

Below the cards you'll find the **Share anonymous usage** switch, the iCloud status, the **Backup** section and the **Credits**. The last three are covered in [Data, iCloud and backups](/apps/trackhobbies/docs/data-sync).
