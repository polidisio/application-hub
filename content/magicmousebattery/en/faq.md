## I don't see my mouse or keyboard

Check that the device is on and connected to your Mac (System Settings → Bluetooth). If none shows up, the panel displays one of these messages:

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| No battery devices found. Make sure your Magic Mouse/Keyboard is connected. | Turn on and connect the device; wait for the next refresh (up to 5 minutes) or close and reopen the panel. |
| Could not access Bluetooth devices. Please check Bluetooth is enabled. | Turn on your Mac's Bluetooth and reopen the panel. |

If the icon is a gray circle with a question mark, the app found no device.

## Does it work with non-Apple keyboards or mice?

It isn't designed for them. It only shows devices that macOS exposes with a battery level in this way, which Apple devices do. We haven't verified other brands.

## The icon's percentage isn't my mouse's

The icon always shows the device with the **lowest** battery. Open the panel to see each one.

## I don't get notifications

1. Open **Settings...**: if you see the yellow warning, enable notifications for MyMouseBattery in System Settings → Notifications.
2. Remember you get a single alert per device until it charges above the threshold plus 2 points. Use **Reset notifications** to test.
3. Check that your threshold is higher than the current level.

More in [Settings and notifications](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/settings).

## The app doesn't open at login

Turn on **Launch at login** in **Settings...** and check that MyMouseBattery is allowed in System Settings → General → Login Items.

## How do I quit the app?

Click the icon and choose **Quit** (⌘Q).

## How often does it update?

Every 60 seconds with the panel open and every 5 minutes with it closed. Opening the panel refreshes immediately. More in [The menu bar and the panel](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/menu-bar).

## Can I see more than 7 days of history?

No. Only the last 7 days are kept. See [Battery history](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/history).

## What data does the app use?

None leaves your Mac. There is no internet connection, no accounts and no analytics. Your settings and the history are stored locally. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).

## Is it paid?

There are no in-app purchases. Check the price on its Mac App Store listing.
