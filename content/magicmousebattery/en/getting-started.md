MyMouseBattery is a macOS menu-bar app that shows the battery level of your wireless Apple devices: Magic Mouse, Magic Keyboard and Magic Trackpad. It has no main window and no Dock icon: it lives only in the menu bar.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| System | macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later |
| Devices | Magic Mouse, Magic Keyboard or Magic Trackpad connected to your Mac |
| Languages | Spanish and English (follows your Mac's language) |
| Distribution | Mac App Store |

## Install and open

1. Install **MyMouseBattery** from the Mac App Store.
2. Open it. No window appears: look for its icon in the menu bar.
3. The first time, macOS asks for permission to send notifications. Accept if you want low-battery alerts (see [Settings and notifications](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/settings)).
4. Click the icon to see the battery of each connected device.

> The app does not ask for Bluetooth permission and has no welcome screen. It reads battery status straight from the system, and only sees devices macOS already has connected.

## What the app does

- Shows the lowest level among your devices in the menu bar, with a battery icon and the percentage.
- Lists each device with its battery in the drop-down panel. See [The menu bar and the panel](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/menu-bar).
- Sends a notification when the battery drops below the threshold you choose.
- Keeps a 7-day battery history. See [Battery history](/apps/magicmousebattery/docs/history).
- Can open itself when you log in.

## Data and privacy

The app uses no internet connection, has no accounts and includes no analytics. The only things it stores, on your Mac, are your settings and the battery history. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
