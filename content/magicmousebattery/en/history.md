The app records each device's battery level and shows it in a chart. It helps you see how long a charge lasts or spot a degraded battery.

## View the history

1. Open the menu-bar panel.
2. Click the chart icon to the right of the device (on hover: **View battery history**).
3. A sheet opens with the device name and a line chart with points. The vertical axis runs from 0 to 100%.
4. Close the sheet with **Close** or Esc.

If the device has no readings yet you will see "No history data yet. Check back later.".

## What is stored

| Aspect | Detail |
|---|---|
| What | One reading (date and percentage) every time the app refreshes the battery |
| For how long | 7 days; older readings are deleted automatically |
| Where | On your Mac, in the app's preferences. It is sent nowhere |
| Per device | Each device has its own history |

## Limitations

- You can't clear the history from the app, nor export it.
- The chart has no labels or zoom: it shows all readings from the last 7 days.
- History is keyed by the device name. If you rename it in Bluetooth, a new history starts.
- Readings are only recorded while the app is running and the device is connected.
