## Does it measure my hydration or alcohol?

No. MyBarTrack counts drinks and spending. It doesn't record volumes, alcohol percentages or alcohol units, and it doesn't calculate blood alcohol.

## "Today's Total" never goes back to zero

It's a running total, not a daily one. To zero it, reset each drink with its arrow, delete history entries or use **Settings → Delete All Consumption History**. To have it clean itself, set a period in **Keep consumption records**.

## I can't log yesterday's drink

The app always saves the date and time of the moment you log. The form's date picker doesn't change that.

## I tapped the reset arrow by mistake

It deletes all entries of that drink without confirmation and can't be undone. If you have a recent CSV export you can look the data up, but there's no way to re-import history.

## I deleted a drink and its history vanished

That's expected: deleting a drink also deletes all of its history.

## I can't edit an entry

Fix it by removing units with **−** or deleting it in History, then add it again.

## The Watch shows nothing

Open the app on the iPhone once. The watch receives its data from there.

## Does it sync with iCloud or Health?

No. Data is local. There are no reminders or notifications either.

## I see Spanish text with my phone in English

A few texts are Spanish-only: the alert when opening a `.json` file, the History date picker title, the "Nombre" placeholder when creating a drink and the widget description. The header counter also says "drink/drinks" in English. It's a known detail of the app.

## What data is sent?

Nothing leaves the device except, **only if you accept**, anonymous usage counts (no drink names, prices or notes). Details in the [privacy policy](/privacy/mybartrack).
