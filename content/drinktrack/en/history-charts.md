Open the history with **History**, at the top right of the main screen.

## Top to bottom

1. **Range**: **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** (default 7). It drives the charts.
2. **Charts** (see below).
3. **Date**: a button showing the date that opens a calendar to choose the day for the list.
4. **Quick filters**: **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Last 7 Days**.
5. **Search** (*Search drinks…*): filters by drink name.
6. **Drink chips**: **All** and one per drink; tap them to filter.
7. **List**: each row shows emoji, name, time, 📝 if it has notes, "x2" if the quantity is above 1 and the total price. Swipe left to delete (no confirmation).

If there are no entries for the chosen date you'll see *No drinks recorded*.

## Charts

- **Total Drinks** and **Total Spending** for the range.
- Only with the 7-day range: **Avg / 7 days** (per day) and **Most drunk** (the most consumed, with its emoji and count).
- **Daily Consumption**: bars per day; orange if that day includes an alcoholic drink, blue otherwise.
- **By Drink Type**: bars per drink.
- **Daily Spending**: spending bars per day.

The range is the last N calendar days, including today. With no data you'll see *No data to display*.

> The **Last 7 Days** list filter follows the range chosen above, so with 30 or 90 days it will show that range.
