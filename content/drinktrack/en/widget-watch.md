## Home Screen widget

Add the **DrinkTrack** widget from the widget gallery (it keeps this name there). It comes in small and medium sizes.

- **Small:** **Today's Total** (the cumulative counter) and cost.
- **Medium:** the above plus the budget bar and its percentage.

It isn't configurable or interactive. The app refreshes it every time something changes.

## Apple Watch app

The iPhone is the source of truth: the watch shows and sends data, but you need to have opened the app on the iPhone at least once (otherwise you'll see *Open MyBarTrack on your iPhone to sync*).

- At the top: counter, cost and budget bar.
- Up to 8 drinks, sorted by usage, shown with emoji only.
- **Tap** a drink to add 1. **Swipe left** to remove 1 from that drink. A button at the bottom undoes the last add made from the watch.
- It vibrates when you pass 100% of the budget.
- Entries from the watch use the drink's base price and the current time.

Sync uses the connection to the iPhone and works without a network: changes made on the watch are sent when it reconnects.

## Watch complication

It's called **MyBarTrack** and comes in three formats: circular (counter and budget ring), rectangular (counter, cost and bar) and inline (for example "3 🍺").
