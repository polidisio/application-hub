The budget is the app's only goal, and it's about **money**, not the number of drinks.

## Setting it up

In **Settings → Budget**:

- **Budget amount**: the figure. At 0 the budget is off.
- **Period**: **Weekly** or **Monthly** (default monthly).

The period starts at the beginning of the week (per your region's first weekday) or the calendar month.

## How it looks

With a budget on, the main screen shows a bar below the summary with "spent / budget" and the percentage.

| Spending | Bar colour |
|---|---|
| Under 80% | Green |
| 80% to 99% | Orange |
| 100% or more | Red |

The bar fills up to a maximum of 150%. The widget and the Apple Watch show the same bar, and the watch vibrates when you pass 100%.

> Unlike the main counter, the budget **does** respect the period: it only counts spending since the start of the current week or month.
