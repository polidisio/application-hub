MyBarTrack is a drink counter with spending control. With one tap you log what you have, see how much you've spent and, if you want, compare it with a weekly or monthly budget. It runs on iPhone and iPad, with a Home Screen widget and an Apple Watch app.

> **A note on the name:** the app was called DrinkTrack during development. On your device, in the App Store and on the Watch it's called **MyBarTrack**. "DrinkTrack" only appears as the widget's name in the widget gallery.

## What it does and doesn't do

**It does:** count drinks, calculate spending, show history and charts, track a budget, export and import data.

**It doesn't:** measure millilitres, alcohol percentage or alcohol units, calculate blood alcohol, set hydration goals, send reminders or connect to Health. The only classification is each drink's category: *Alcohol* or *Non-Alcoholic*.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS 16.0 or later (portrait only on iPhone).
- Apple Watch with watchOS 10.0 or later, if you want the watch app.
- English and Spanish, following the system language.
- The currency is your device's (€ if it can't be determined). It can't be changed inside the app.

## First launch

There's no tutorial. The first time you open the app a sheet appears, **Help improve MyBarTrack**, asking whether to share anonymous usage counts (no drink names, prices or personal data).

- **Share anonymous usage** turns analytics on.
- **Not now** leaves it off.

You can't dismiss it without choosing. Change it any time in **Settings → Analytics**.

The app comes with six example drinks:

| Drink | Starting price | Category |
|---|---|---|
| 🍺 Beer | 3.50 | Alcohol |
| 🥤 Soda | 2.00 | Non-Alcoholic |
| 💧 Water | 1.50 | Non-Alcoholic |
| 🍷 Wine | 4.00 | Alcohol |
| 🍸 Shot | 6.00 | Alcohol |
| ☕ Coffee | 1.80 | Non-Alcoholic |

## The main screen

There's no tab bar: everything revolves around one screen with pop-up sheets.

- **Top left:** a share button that exports your drink list (see [Data and backups](/apps/drinktrack/docs/data)).
- **Top right:** the gear (**Settings**) and the **History** text button.
- **Summary card:** **Today's Total** (number of drinks) and **Cost**, with the budget bar below if you've set one. Careful: "Today's Total" adds up everything logged, not just today. See [Logging drinks](/apps/drinktrack/docs/log-drinks).
- **Drink list:** one card per drink, from most to least consumed.
- **Orange bottom button:** **Add Drink**.
