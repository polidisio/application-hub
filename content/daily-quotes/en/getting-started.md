Daily Quotes is a web page showing a **quote of the day** inside a window that imitates Windows 95/98 WordPad. No accounts, no sign-up, no install: open the site and read the quote.

## Requirements

- Any modern browser with JavaScript enabled. It's a web page, not an app: nothing to install.
- An internet connection to load it.

## Open the site

Go to [daily-quotes.saraiba.eu](https://daily-quotes.saraiba.eu/). After half a second you'll see the quote, the author and the date. You don't have to click anything.

## What's on screen

| Area | Contents |
|---|---|
| Title bar | `citas.txt - WordPad`, with the window buttons (`_`, `□`, `×`). |
| Paper | The quote in italics on a yellow background, the author (`— Author`) and a *Quote of …* line with today's date. |
| Status bar | Save status, `Ln 1, Col 1`, `100%`, `CP1252` and today's date. |
| Desktop icons | Four icons on the left (My Computer, Recycle Bin, My Documents, Internet). |

## Language

The page picks its language from your browser's language: if it starts with `es` you get Spanish quotes and texts; anything else gets English. There is no switch: change your browser's language instead.

Note that the menu bar (*Archivo*, *Editar*, *Ver*, *Insertar*, *Formato*, *Ayuda*) and the window title `citas.txt` are always in Spanish.

## What does nothing

It's retro decoration only. The menus, the toolbar buttons, the window buttons and the desktop icons don't react to clicks. To see how the quote is chosen, read [How it works](/apps/daily-quotes/docs/how-it-works).
