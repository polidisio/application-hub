QIResizer is a macOS utility that resizes images in batches and converts them to JPEG, PNG or HEIC. You drag in your images, pick a size and a format, and save the result to the folder you choose. Everything is processed on your Mac: there are no accounts and no connection is needed.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later.
- The interface is **English only**. This guide quotes the labels exactly as they appear (*Browse Files*, *Resize Now*, *Lock Aspect Ratio*...).
- The app runs in the macOS sandbox and can only access the files you drop or pick and the destination folder you select.

## Your first resize

1. Open QIResizer. The window has a minimum size of 600 × 650.
2. Add images: drag them onto the **Drop your images here** area or click **Browse Files**.
3. Under **Dimensions**, choose a value in **Size** (default **1080p**).
4. Under **Output Settings**, choose the **Format** (default **JPEG**) and, if it applies, the **Quality** (default 85%).
5. Click **Resize Now**.
6. Pick the destination folder in the panel that opens (title *Choose a folder to save resized images*, button **Select Folder**).
7. Wait for the progress bar to finish. The space saved appears at the bottom.

> Your original images are never modified: the result is always saved as a new file.

## Tour of the window

| Area | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Header | The **QIResizer** name and a round button to change the theme. |
| Drop area | Drag images, **Browse Files**, and once images are added, **Add** and **Clear**. |
| **Dimensions** | The **Size** picker, the custom width and **Lock Aspect Ratio**. |
| **Output Settings** | **Format**, **Custom Name** and the **Quality** slider. |
| Footer | Progress and **Cancel** while working; otherwise statistics and **Resize Now**. |
