Tapping a source on **Sources** opens a screen titled with the source's name. From there you reach your channels.

## Source screen

Each row shows its name and, on the right, how many items it contains:

| Row | Content |
|---|---|
| **Continue watching** | Only shown if there are unfinished movies or episodes. See [Movies and series](/apps/streamaura/docs/movies-series). |
| **Movies** | Only if the source has movies. |
| **Series** | Only if the source has series. |
| **All channels** | Every live channel, in playlist order. |
| **Favorites** | Your favorite channels from this source. |
| Groups | One group per playlist category (`group-title`), in the order they appear. Channels without a category go to **No group**. |

If the source has no content yet you see **No content** with the text *Refresh the source to import the channels.*

## Channel list

Each channel shows its logo (or a TV icon if it has none), its number if the playlist provides one, its name and its group. A yellow star marks favorites. Tap a channel to play it.

When you play from a list, you can switch to the previous or next channel without going back (see [Player](/apps/streamaura/docs/player)). The zapping list covers the 250 channels before and the 250 after the one you tapped.

> Each list shows at most **3000 channels**. If there are more you see the note *Showing the first 3000 channels. Use search to narrow down.* Search is also limited to 3000 results.

## Search channels

On the source screen, the **Search channels** field filters by name across **all** channels of that source (case- and accent-insensitive). If nothing matches you see **No results**. Search is per source: it doesn't search other sources or your library.

## Favorites

- **Add:** press and hold a channel (on Apple TV, press and hold the center button of the remote) and choose **Add to favorites**.
- **Remove:** press and hold the channel, in any list, and choose **Remove from favorites**.
- **View:** open **Favorites** on the source screen. If it's empty you see **No favorites** and *Press and hold a channel to add it.*

Favorites are per source and are shown in playlist order, not in the order you added them. They are stored only on your device. Favorite movies and series are a separate list (**Favorites**) inside **Movies** and **Series**.
