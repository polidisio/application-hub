StreamAura is a player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV that brings your IPTV playlists (M3U/M3U8), your Xtream Codes accounts and the video library on your NAS or media server together in one place. There are no accounts or sign-up: open the app, add your sources and play.

> StreamAura **does not include, host or recommend channels, playlists or content**. You provide your own sources and are responsible for having the right to play them. The app says so in **Settings > About > Legal notices**.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later, or Apple TV with tvOS 17.0 or later.
- The app is available in English and Spanish and follows your device language.
- The app always uses a dark theme, even if your device is in light mode.
- You need a connection to download playlists, use Xtream accounts and play. There is no offline downloading.
- To read a NAS on your local network, the app asks for **local network** permission the first time. If you decline, it cannot find or read your server; you can turn it on later in the system settings.

## First launch

There is no tutorial, welcome sheet or analytics prompt: StreamAura does not collect usage data (see [Data and privacy](/apps/streamaura/docs/data-privacy)). On first launch you see the empty **Sources** screen with the message **No sources yet**: *Add an M3U or M3U8 playlist, or an Xtream Codes account, to get started.*

Two buttons are available:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add source** | Opens **New source** to add an IPTV playlist or an Xtream account. See [IPTV playlists and Xtream accounts](/apps/streamaura/docs/iptv-sources). |
| **Add media server (NAS)** | Opens **Media server** to connect your library. See [My library (NAS)](/apps/streamaura/docs/my-library). |

## Tour of the app

There is no tab bar. Everything starts from **Sources**, the root screen:

- Top right: the gear icon (**Settings**) and the **+** button (**Add**), which opens a menu with **Add IPTV playlist** and **Add media server (NAS)**.
- **My library**: shown at the top only once you have added a media server, with rows of movies and series.
- Below, one card per IPTV playlist or Xtream account. Tap it to open it.

Inside a source:

| Entry | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Continue watching** | Movies and episodes you started and haven't finished (only shown if there are any). |
| **Movies** and **Series** | The catalog, if the source has one. |
| **All channels** | Every live channel. |
| **Favorites** | Your favorite channels. |
| Groups | One group per category in the playlist, e.g. *News* or *Sports*. |
