Cards are managed from the deck's study screen (tap a deck to open it).

## Adding a card

1. Open a deck. If it's empty you'll see **No Cards in Deck** and the **Add First Card** button.
2. In a deck that already has cards, use the **+** button in the top bar or the **Add Card** button below the card.
3. Fill in **Front (Question)** (*Enter question*) and **Back (Answer)** (*Enter answer*). Each field accepts several lines.
4. Tap **Add**. It's disabled until both fields have text. **Cancel** discards the card.

The new card is added to the end of the deck.

> Cards are **text only**. The data model allows images and audio, but the app offers no way to add them, neither when creating cards nor when importing.

## Editing a card

There are two ways:

- Press and hold the card and choose **Edit Card**.
- Tap the **Edit** button below the card.

In the **Edit Card** sheet, change the front and the back. Tap **Done** to save and close. There is no cancel button: changes are saved when you tap **Done**.

> **Known issue:** the **Edit** button opens the card at that position in the full deck. In **Due Today** mode, where cards are reordered, it may open a different card from the one you see. To be safe, use the press-and-hold menu on the card.

## Deleting a card

- Press and hold the card and choose **Delete Card**, or
- open **Edit Card** and tap **Delete**.

> There is no confirmation and it can't be undone.

## Limits

- There is no bulk editor and no manual reordering.
- To create many cards at once, use [Importing decks](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/import).
- You can't move a card from one deck to another.
