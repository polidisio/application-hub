## My decks or my progress have disappeared

This is a current limitation of the app: on launch it always reloads the four sample decks and your own decks and statistics are lost. See [Data and storage](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/data-storage). If you still have the original CSV or Excel file, you can [import it](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/import) again.

## Can I add images or audio to a card?

No. Cards are text only; the app has no button to attach images or audio.

## Are there "Hard" and "Easy" buttons?

No. There are only **Correct** and **Incorrect**. See [Spaced repetition](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/spaced-repetition).

## "Due Today" shows me every card

That's expected today: that mode reorders the whole deck (due cards first) but doesn't hide the others. See [Studying](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/study).

## I can't change a deck's colour or move cards between decks

Correct: the colour is chosen when the deck is created and only the name can be changed. To change anything else, create a new deck.

## I deleted a deck or card by mistake

There is no confirmation and no undo. If the deck came from a file, import it again.

## I can't reset statistics

The statistics screen has no button for it in this version. See [Statistics](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/stats).

## Searching for a deck opens a different one

This is a known issue: with text in the search field, actions may affect another deck. Clear the search before opening, renaming or deleting.

## Import fails or cards come out misplaced

Check that the CSV is UTF-8, that each card is on one line and that there are two columns (front and back). For Excel, use the first sheet and plain text cells. See [Importing decks](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/import).

## Some labels appear in English

Part of the interface (the mode selector, the statistics badges and the import screen) shows in English even when your device is in another language.

## Does the app tell me when to study?

No. There are no notifications or reminders, and the app asks for no permissions.

## Does it work in dark mode or offline?

Offline, always. Dark mode, no: the app always uses the light theme.

## Does it sync with another device or iCloud?

No. Data lives only on the device.

## What data is sent?

None. The app uses no network, accounts or analytics. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
