Tap the gear in the top-left corner of the main screen to open **Settings**. Close it with **Done**.

## Options

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| **Font size** | 8 to 28 points, with a stepper (default 14). |
| **Colors** | **Classic** (black with light gray text), **Green** (black with green text), **Solarized** (dark blue with blue-gray text) and **Light** (white with black text). |

Below the options there's a preview (`user@host:~$ ls -la`) showing the chosen font and colors.

> These settings apply to all sessions and are remembered between uses. They can only be changed from the main screen, not inside the terminal.

## What's not there

There's no language setting (the interface is English only), no analytics setting and no sync setting: the app collects nothing. See [Data and privacy](/apps/noduscli/docs/data-privacy).
