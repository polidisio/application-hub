Each deck has its own statistics screen. Open it from the study screen with the bar chart icon (top right). Close it with **Done**. The circular arrows button refreshes the data.

## Summary

Four boxes:

| Box | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Total Cards** | Number of cards in the deck. |
| **Mastered** | Cards with level 6 or higher. See [Spaced repetition](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/spaced-repetition). |
| **Avg. Precision** | Average precision of the cards you've already studied (unstudied cards don't count). Green from 75%, orange from 50 to 75%, red below. |
| **Sessions** | Activity counter for the deck (see the note). |

> **Sessions** are not real sessions. It increases every time you rate a card and also every time you tap **Done** on the study screen, even if you studied nothing.

## Study Streak

Shows the streak days next to a flame and the **Last studied** date. The streak goes up by 1 when you study between one and two days after the last time (counted in 24-hour blocks), and goes back to 1 if more than two days pass. Studying several times on the same day doesn't change it.

## Card Statistics

A list of every card in the deck, from most to least studied. Each row shows the front, the back and, on the right, the level (**Lv**), the precision percentage and the times studied. Level colours: red from 0 to 3, orange from 4 to 6, green from 7 to 9 and dark teal at 10.

## Statistics on the study screen

Under each card you see its five values: **Level**, **Precision**, **Studied**, **Correct** and **Incorrect**.

## Resetting statistics

The code includes a **Reset Statistics** alert (*Are you sure you want to reset all statistics for this deck? This action cannot be undone.*), but the screen has no button that opens it. For now you can't reset statistics from the app.

> Statistics, like the rest of your data, are currently not kept when you close the app. See [Data and storage](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/data-storage).
