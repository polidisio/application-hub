A deck is a collection of cards with a name and a colour. You manage them all from **My Decks**.

> Remember: right now the decks you create don't survive closing the app. See [Data and storage](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/data-storage).

## Creating a deck

1. On **My Decks**, tap the **+** button (top right).
2. In **New Deck**, type the name in **Deck Name** (*Enter deck name*).
3. Pick a **Color** from the 11 available: red, orange, yellow, green, mint, teal, blue, indigo, purple, pink and brown. The default is blue.
4. Tap **Save**. The button is disabled while the name is empty. **Cancel** discards the deck.

The new deck appears at the end of the list and starts empty. To add cards, see [Cards](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/cards).

## What each deck shows

Each deck in the list has a bar in its colour, its name and its card count (for example "10 cards"). The colour is also used on the study screen.

## Searching decks

Type in **Search decks** to filter by name (case-insensitive). It only searches deck names, not card contents.

> **Needs review:** while there is text in the search field, actions on a deck (opening, renaming, deleting) may apply to a different deck of the full list, because the app uses the position in the filtered list. Clear the search before opening, renaming or deleting a deck.

## Renaming or deleting a deck

Press and hold a deck to open its context menu:

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Rename** | Opens a sheet with **Deck Name**. **Save** applies the change (disabled if the name is empty); **Cancel** discards it. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the deck with all its cards and statistics. |

> **Delete** asks for no confirmation and can't be undone. A deck's colour can't be changed after it's created.
