ThinkDeck Study is an app for studying with **flashcards** organised into **decks**. Each card has a front (question) and a back (answer); you flip it, decide whether you got it right, and the app schedules when you'll see it again using spaced repetition. There are no accounts or sign-up: open the app and study.

> **Important, current state of the app:** every time it starts, the app reloads its four sample decks. Decks you create or import, cards you add or edit, and your statistics **are not kept after you close and reopen the app** (you can use them while the app stays open). Details in [Data and storage](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/data-storage).

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later.
- The app is available in Spanish, English, French and German, and uses your device language.
- It needs no internet connection and no special permissions (no access to photos, microphone, notifications or location).
- The app always uses **light mode**, even if your device is in dark mode. On iPhone it only works in portrait; on iPad it rotates to any orientation.

## First launch

There is no welcome screen and no permission prompt. When you open the app you'll see **My Decks** with four sample decks of 10 cards each:

| Deck | Colour |
|---|---|
| Vocabulary | Red |
| History | Blue |
| Science | Green |
| Languages | Purple |

These decks' names and contents are in English, in every app language.

## App tour

The app has a single main screen, **My Decks**, with no tab bar:

| Element | What it does |
|---|---|
| **My Decks** title | Underneath it shows the totals, for example "4 decks • 40 cards". |
| **Search decks** field | Filters the list by name. |
| Import button (down arrow, top left) | Creates a deck from a CSV or Excel file. See [Importing decks](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/import). |
| **+** button (top right) | Creates a new deck. See [Decks](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/decks). |
| Each deck in the list | Tap it to open that deck's study screen. |

There is no settings screen. To start studying, see [Studying](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/study).

## Your first study session in a minute

1. Tap a deck, for example *Vocabulary*.
2. Tap the card to flip it and see the answer.
3. Tap **Correct** if you got it or **Incorrect** if you missed it.
4. The app moves to the next card and schedules when you'll see it again.
5. Tap **Done** to leave.
