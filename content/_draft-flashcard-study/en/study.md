Tap a deck on **My Decks** to open its study screen. It opens as a sheet over the list; close it with **Done**.

## The study screen

From top to bottom:

- **Done** (top left), and on the right the chart icon (deck statistics) and **+** (add card).
- A selector with two study modes (see below).
- The **Card X of Y** counter.
- The card, in the deck's colour.
- Below the card, five badges with that card's data: **Level**, **Precision**, **Studied**, **Correct** and **Incorrect**.
- The rating buttons, the navigation arrows, and the **Add Card** and **Edit** buttons.

## A session step by step

1. Read the front of the card and try to recall the answer.
2. Tap the card to flip it (*Tap card to reveal answer*).
3. Rate your answer with one of the two buttons:

| Button | When to use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Incorrect** (red, with an X) | You didn't know it or got it wrong. | Counts as a miss, lowers the card's level and schedules it to come back in 1 day. |
| **Correct** (green, with a check) | You knew it. | Counts as a hit, raises the level and lengthens the interval until the next review. |

4. The app moves to the next card automatically.

On iPhone, the rating buttons only appear after you flip the card. On iPad they are always visible. The exact formula is in [Spaced repetition](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/spaced-repetition).

> There are only two ratings. There are no buttons such as "Hard" or "Easy".

## Study modes

The selector at the top has two options: **All Cards** and **Due Today**.

| Mode | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **All Cards** | Every card in the deck, in the deck's own order (they are not shuffled). |
| **Due Today** | Every card in the deck sorted by priority: first the ones that are due (new and overdue, most overdue first), then the ones that aren't due yet. |

> **Limitation:** **Due Today** doesn't hide cards that aren't due yet; it only puts them at the end. A card that isn't due today still stays in the session. The order is also recalculated after each rating (the card you just rated moves to the end), so as you advance you may skip a card. If that happens, use the arrows to go back.

## Moving around without rating

The left and right arrows below the card go to the previous or next card without rating. They are disabled on the first and last card. When you rate the last card, the session goes back to the first one: there is no end-of-session screen. To finish, tap **Done**.

## Gestures

- **Tap** the card: flip it.
- **Press and hold** the card: menu with **Edit Card** and **Delete Card**.
- Deck statistics: chart icon. See [Statistics](/apps/flashcard-study/docs/stats).
