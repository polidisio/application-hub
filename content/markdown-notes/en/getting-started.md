Markdown Notes is an iOS notepad where you write in Markdown and see the formatted result. There are no accounts or sign-up: open the app and start writing.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 17.0 or later.
- The app is available in Spanish and English. It uses Spanish when your device is set to Spanish and English otherwise.
- iCloud is optional. Without iCloud the app works the same and your notes stay on the device. More in [Data and sync](/apps/markdown-notes/docs/data-sync).

> Some interface texts (for example the **Notas** heading, the search field, **Eliminar** or **Cancelar**) always appear in Spanish, even when your device is in English. This is a limitation of the current version.

## First launch

There is no welcome screen, no sample notes and no permission prompt. You will see an empty list and, on the right (or after opening a note on iPhone), the message **Selecciona una nota** (shown in Spanish).

1. Tap **New note** (pencil-on-square icon) in the top bar of the list.
2. A note called *New note* is created with the content `# New note`, and it opens in the editor.
3. Change the title in the **Title** field and type your text below.

There is no save button: the note saves itself every time you type.

## App tour

The app has two areas. On iPhone they show one after the other (open a note, go back with the back arrow); on iPad you see both at once.

| Area | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **List** (sidebar) | **All Notes**, **Folders**, the notes list, search, and the import/export menu. |
| **Editor** | Title, edit modes, tags, formatting bar, text and counters. |

## What it does not include

So you do not go looking: the app has **no** focus mode, templates, trash, pinned notes or keyboard shortcuts. There is also no purchase or subscription screen inside the app.

## Privacy

The app has no analytics or ads and does not send your notes to any server of ours. The only copy outside your device is the one in your iCloud, if you have it enabled. See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
